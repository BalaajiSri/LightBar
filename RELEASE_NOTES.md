## Download

Download **LightBar-macOS-universal.zip** below, unzip it, move **LightBar.app** to **Applications**, and open it.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Includes Apple Silicon and Intel executables; runtime checks ran on Apple Silicon. Intel runtime is not yet hardware-tested.

## What changed

- Redesigned dark interface with connection, colour, and music panels.
- Custom macOS app icon.
- One adaptive music mode combining frequency energy, bass accents, and stereo balance.
- Live bass, midrange, and treble meters.
- Built-in lamp animations accessible from a separate menu.

Music streaming currently controls the whole bar. Individual regions are not supported. Midrange analysis does not isolate vocals.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. macOS may require Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. Allow Bluetooth and Screen & System Audio Recording for the relevant features. An app update can require removing and re-adding its recording permission entry; always use the installed copy in Applications.

Protocol frames, synthetic frequency/stereo analysis, universal architectures, and the app signature were checked. The interface was rendered and visually inspected. Physical light and music response remain dependent on the device.
